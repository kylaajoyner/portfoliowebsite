Client: Old Spice “Pure Sport”
Problem:
Old Spice needed a way to capture a key target audience of young men ages 18–24 for their “Pure Sport” line of deodorant while still maintaining brand authenticity and noticeability.
Solution:
My design draws on vintage gym branding, a trending aesthetic for Gen Z & millennial young men.
Role:
Designer

Drag to spin

Awards: Chosen as Best Design by Old Spice CFO

  • Stripes

    The two lines resemble the stripes of athletic socks while drawing the eye to the “Pure Sport”.

  • Wheel

    Resembles a captain’s steering wheel and a barbell plate.

  • Stars

    Represents heritage, pride and valor. Combined with the Old Spice plate, it creates the classic Old Spice patch.
